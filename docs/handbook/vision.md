# VISION

> **Establish the reference.**
> **Build with intention.**
> **Leave the workshop better than you found it.**

---

`Build software that people learn from.`

The philosophy of Perpend explains **why** the project exists.

Its vision describes **what we hope it becomes**.

This is not a roadmap.

It is not a list of planned features.

It is a description of the kind of project we aspire to build, the values we hope to preserve, and the impact we hope Perpend will have over time.

---

## OUR ASPIRATION

We hope Perpend becomes a reference implementation of intentional software engineering.

Its first implementation happens to be a Neovim distribution.

Its greater purpose is to demonstrate how software can be designed with clarity, discipline, and long-term thinking.

We hope developers recommend Perpend not because it has the most features, but because it demonstrates thoughtful engineering.

Not because it provides every workflow.

But because it teaches a way of building software that remains understandable years after it was written.

---

## BEYOND THE EDITOR

Although Perpend is built around Neovim, we hope its ideas remain valuable far beyond the editor itself.

The principles explored throughout the project—modular architecture, clear responsibilities, deliberate documentation, architectural decision records, and maintainable organization—apply to software engineering as a whole.

The editor is the medium.

Engineering is the objective.

If those ideas encourage developers to improve projects outside of Perpend, then the project has already fulfilled a purpose greater than its original scope.

---

## A LIVING BODY OF KNOWLEDGE

We hope the handbook grows into a lasting engineering resource.

Not simply documentation for contributors, but a collection of ideas, architectural decisions, research, experiments, and lessons learned throughout the life of the project.

The code explains implementation.

The handbook preserves understanding.

Together they become a record of how thoughtful software is built.

---

## A THOUGHTFUL COMMUNITY

We hope contributors can understand the project before they modify it.

Clear architecture, documented decisions, and consistent engineering practices should make meaningful contributions approachable without sacrificing quality.

Growth should never come at the expense of clarity.

The goal is not to grow quickly.

The goal is to grow deliberately.

---

## LOOKING AHEAD

Technology changes.

Tools evolve.

Languages improve.

Frameworks rise and fall.

The principles of good engineering endure.

Whatever Perpend becomes in the future, we hope it continues to value:

- understanding before implementation,
- clarity over unnecessary complexity,
- craftsmanship over convenience,
- and deliberate design over accidental growth.

If those values remain visible in every architectural decision, every document, and every line of code, then Perpend will continue to serve its purpose.

Not merely as a project.

But as a workshop where engineers learn to think deliberately.
